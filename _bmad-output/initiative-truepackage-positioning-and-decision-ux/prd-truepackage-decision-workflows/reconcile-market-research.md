# Market Research Reconciliation

Compared the [market research](../../research-truepackage-india-to-germany-offer-tool/research-truepackage-india-to-germany-offer-tool.md), PRD, and [data addendum](addendum.md).

## Findings

- **Competitor saturation:** the research found net-pay, PPP, India/Germany, and city-comparison tools already available. The PRD should not imply those ingredients are unique; it should position the integrated workflow as a hypothesis.
- **LLM differentiation:** the PRD preserves workflow and evidence visibility, not an unsupported accuracy claim. Add a controlled same-scenario comparison measuring time, missing inputs, assumptions, and usability.
- **User voice remains weak:** Reddit access failed and no interviews were conducted. Anecdotes are labeled; validate segment needs before treating journeys as representative.
- **Current trust defects need explicit acceptance criteria:** the live share action does not serialize changed inputs; the 52–57% headline conflicts with the 49% example; decision copy can mix “Accept” and “Counter”; the footer says June 2026 while the source register says March 2026.
- **Student scope is expanded:** a multi-factor shortlist comparison is now requested by the owner, supported by candidate sources in the addendum. It is not evidence of broader demand, and the default weights remain a validation gate.

No segment priority, student weighting, accuracy superiority, or paid demand is validated by this research.
