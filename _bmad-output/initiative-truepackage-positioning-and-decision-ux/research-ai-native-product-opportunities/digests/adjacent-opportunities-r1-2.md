## Opportunity assessment

- **Student program/cost/outcome comparison** remains a promising adjacency because products and source systems exist, but this scan found no recent verified user evidence that the combined comparison is a must-have or that students would pay for it. Not validated as a standalone product.
- **Relocation finance coordination** (banking, transfers, paperwork, tax deadlines) may be a workflow gap, but this pass did not establish a current unmet need or differentiation from existing financial providers. Keep as an idea for discovery, not a build recommendation.

## Limits

No market size, conversion, retention, or willingness-to-pay evidence was retrieved. The observed products describe features and availability, not user preference.
