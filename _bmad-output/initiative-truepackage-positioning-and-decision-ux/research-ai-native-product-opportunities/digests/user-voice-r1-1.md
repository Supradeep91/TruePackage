# User-voice digest, round 1

**Accessed:** 2026-10-30  
**Question:** What recent evidence describes the decision users are trying to make and the workarounds they use?

## Findings

- **[claim, source, publisher, pub_date, accessed, confidence, class]** NDTV reported on a senior software engineer in India comparing a ₹45L+ salary with an €80k Berlin offer; the quoted question asks how to compare and decide. Article-reported replies raise take-home pay, rent/savings, family costs, startup risk, and non-financial reasons to move. ## Limits and leads

Original Reddit posts were blocked; forum claims are single cases and not representative. No accessible recent first-person evidence was verified for comparing German programs on internships, safety, course quality, or community; no reliable recent evidence showed that an LLM or calculator helped users decide. Do not infer demand prevalence or willingness to pay. Next evidence should be moderated interviews and same-scenario testing with one participant from each intended segment, then a larger segment-specific sample.
