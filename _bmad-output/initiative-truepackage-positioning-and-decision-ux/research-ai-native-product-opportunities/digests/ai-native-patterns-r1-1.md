# AI-native product pattern digest, round 1

**Accessed:** 2026-10-30  
**Question:** Which AI patterns could strengthen TruePackage's guided decision workflow, and what evidence exists beyond novelty?

## Findings

- **[claim, source, publisher, pub_date, accessed, confidence, class]** LinkedIn documents natural-language job search that interprets intent across job descriptions, may personalize using profile/search activity, proposes filters, and accepts feedback; the page also acknowledges results may not match. This is a pattern for guided intent intake and correction, not proof it improves decisions. **Source:** https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a6889044; **publisher:** LinkedIn; **pub_date:** approximately 2026-09 (page said “1 month ago”); **accessed:** 2026-10-30; **confidence:** high for documented capability; **class:** AI product feature.
- **[claim, source, publisher, pub_date, accessed, confidence, class]** TurboTax announced AI document intake that interpreted uploaded tax documents, created a personalized checklist, checked completeness, and offered access to a human tax expert. The announcement is from 2023, so current availability and outcomes were not established. **Source:** https://blog.turbotax.intuit.com/breaking-news/tax-preparation-powered-by-gen-ai-59318; **publisher:** TurboTax / Intuit; **pub_date:** 2023-09-06, updated 2023-09-19; **accessed:** 2026-10-30; **confidence:** high for what was announced, low for current status; **class:** historical product announcement.
- **[claim, source, publisher, pub_date, accessed, confidence, class]** Credit Karma announced an AI conversation using financial data to suggest cash-flow and spending options, but described some capabilities as forward-looking. Shipping status and user impact were not verified. **Source:** https://www.creditkarma.com/about/releases/introducing-intuit-assist-for-credit-karma; **publisher:** Intuit Credit Karma; **pub_date:** 2023-09-06; **accessed:** 2026-10-30; **confidence:** high for announcement, low for current status; **class:** historical product announcement.

## Opportunity hypothesis

Test an **offer-to-decision guide**: accept or extract offer details, turn them into an editable checklist, ask only for missing scenario inputs, calculate scenarios with the deterministic tax/cost engine, and use AI to explain trade-offs with links to sources and assumptions. Keep model-generated interpretation distinct from calculated figures. The source set supports the component patterns (intent intake, document checklist, feedback/correction), but not conversion lift, demand, or user trust.

## Limits

No recent independent user evidence tied to these AI features or measurable outcomes was found. Reddit results were not feature-specific; G2 content could not be retrieved. Do not claim AI improves results until tested with representative tasks and users.
