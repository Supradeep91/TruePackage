## Opportunity hypothesis

A TruePackage AI bet could be **guided intake and explanation around a deterministic decision engine**: convert a natural-language or uploaded offer into editable inputs; ask only for missing fields; calculate using the existing tax/cost logic; explain trade-offs with source-linked statements; and accept corrections. LLM-generated text must stay separate from numeric results. This combines current LinkedIn intent input with older Intuit checklist/expert handoff patterns. Evidence supports the patterns, not demand or conversion lift for TruePackage.

## Verification limits

No recent independent user evidence tied to these features or measurable impact was found. LinkedIn itself says results may not match. Do not frame personalization as correctness or efficacy.
