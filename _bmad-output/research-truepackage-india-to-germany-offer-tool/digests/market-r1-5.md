## Product audit (internal evidence, not market evidence)

- The public home page at https://truepackage.vercel.app/ was inspected on 2026-10-30. At a 1024x640 viewport, the centered hero consumes most of the first screen; the calculator narrative and inputs begin near the lower edge, with the personal decision verdict further down. The hero says “Your offer looks great. Until tax hits” and uses a generic subhead (“Built for Indians navigating Germany…”), while the calculator later says the more differentiated “Germany Number.”
- The first-screen headline claims Germans keep 52–57% of gross, while the visible default example says 49% reaches the account. The decision card can render “Good offer — counter to €100K” alongside “Accept. Counter to €100K if there is room.” The two action verbs weaken clarity even though the underlying number is concrete.
- The product's value-proposition document [docs/decisions/001-value-proposition.md] predates these observed direct competitors and claims their personalized parity outcome is the core distinction. Current competitor pages mean “cross-country comparison” and “a number for whether Germany makes financial sense” are no longer exclusive category language.
