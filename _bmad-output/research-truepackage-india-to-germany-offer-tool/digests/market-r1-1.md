# Market / alternatives digest, round 1

**Accessed:** 2026-10-30  
**Research question:** What does an Indian professional evaluating German pay get from existing tools, and how close are they to a personal move/offer decision?

## Findings

- **[claim, source, publisher, pub_date, accessed, confidence, class]** Relocate.me's Germany calculator lets a user set gross annual income, currency, and tax class, then displays annual/monthly net; its page links to country guides, jobs, cost of living, salaries and visas, and carries an informational-use disclaimer. It does not claim an India-specific parity or offer-counter recommendation on the calculator page. **Source:** https://relocate.me/taxes/calculator/germany; **publisher:** Relocate.me; **pub_date:** 2026-04-17 (page's last update); **accessed:** 2026-10-30; **confidence:** high for observed page features; **class:** competitor capability.
- **[claim, source, publisher, pub_date, accessed, confidence, class]** dual.tax offers a directly relevant India-to-Germany tax comparison: net pay for both countries, effective tax rates, and a purchasing-power/cost-of-living adjustment. The page labels the estimate generalized/educational and notes that housing and lifestyle vary by city; the observed flow does not present a personal accept/counter salary threshold. **Source:** https://dual.tax/tools/tax-calculator/in/germany; **publisher:** dual.tax; **pub_date:** 2026-09-24 (FX source date shown; page itself lists FY 2026-27); **accessed:** 2026-10-30; **confidence:** high for observed page claims; **class:** competitor capability.
- **[claim, source, publisher, pub_date, accessed, confidence, class]** Indian in Germany's calculator shows German net pay and deductions in INR, an Indian CTC equivalent, estimated monthly savings after typical living costs, Blue Card eligibility, and salary ranges for Indian IT roles. This is a close audience-specific substitute, although the visible framing emphasizes salary explanation and typical savings rather than a personalized India-vs-Germany parity decision. **Source:** https://indianingermany.com/tools/salary-calculator; **publisher:** Indian in Germany; **pub_date:** 2026 (page contains 2026 rates and ranges; no exact publication date shown); **accessed:** 2026-10-30; **confidence:** high for observed page features, medium for absence of personalized parity recommendation; **class:** competitor capability.
- **[claim, source, publisher, pub_date, accessed, confidence, class]** SalaryAfterTax's Germany page supports gross-to-net calculation, German tax options, salary insights/local median comparison, and comparisons of take-home across 18 other countries. It is broader than a German-only calculator, but does not frame the result as a personalized relocation decision in the inspected page. **Source:** https://salaryaftertax.com/de/salary-calculator; **publisher:** SalaryAfterTax; **pub_date:** 2026-09 (page says last updated September 2026); **accessed:** 2026-10-30; **confidence:** high for observed features; **class:** competitor capability.

## Leads and gaps

- Investigate dual.tax's actual interactive inputs and decision outputs; the page's country-level purchasing-power comparison is the closest substitute.
- Investigate whether Indian in Germany has separate offer-decision tools and whether its calculator personalizes by city/family/remittance.
- User-voice evidence has not yet been acquired; search engines must be accessed through browser-rendered Bing because Google page fetch is challenged.
