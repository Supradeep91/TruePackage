# Product Brief Reconciliation

Compared the [Product Brief](../brief-truepackage-positioning/brief-truepackage-positioning.md) with the PRD and its [addendum](addendum.md). This records alignment and changes; it makes no new product decisions.

## Findings

- **Student scope expanded and reconciled.** The brief originally described upfront study funds and a monthly gap. The owner approved updating it to include comparison of shortlisted programs across course fit, outcomes, affordability, work/internships, safety, and community. This scope is now aligned with PRD FR-6–FR-9; default scoring remains a pre-launch validation gate, and the factors are not validated user priorities.
- **Data freshness expanded.** The brief calls for visible dates and identifies a site/source-register mismatch. The PRD generalizes provenance and adds student-specific data coverage; this note asks that the known date mismatch be an explicit acceptance criterion.
- **Trust and comprehension preserved.** The brief's success order remains baseline comprehension/trust, then per-journey traction. Numeric targets remain deferred until baseline testing.
- **Scenario sharing preserved and specified.** The owner approved fragment-based sharing with preview/warning, no personal identifiers, and no analytics payload values. Browser-history and clear-link safeguards remain open in PRD §9.
- **Four journeys and no lead segment preserved.** Segment selection remains a later evidence-based decision. LLM differentiation stays a hypothesis, not a claim that LLMs cannot calculate. Monetization follows traction.
