## Opportunity implication

A standalone “AI university finder/ranker” is not a whitespace opportunity by itself. Existing competitors already offer weighted destination ranking, AI matching, or side-by-side cost/quality comparisons. A more defensible product bet would focus on a traceable decision method: source-level provenance, dated evidence, comparable-factor coverage, user-adjustable weights, and a visible “insufficient data” outcome. No evidence yet shows that this differentiated approach wins users or supports payment.
